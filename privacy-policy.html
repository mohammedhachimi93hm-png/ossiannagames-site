2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information through our Games. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal details, and the Games do not require an account.

The Games do not send usage statistics, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, crash reports or any other information to us or to third parties. We do not run servers for the Games.

3. Data stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, each Game saves your progress (for example levels completed, stars, coins earned and settings) only on your own device. This information never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the Game, or clearing its data in your device settings, deletes it.

4. No ads, no purchases, no tracking

  • No advertising: our Games show no ads and contain no advertising software.
  • No in-app purchases: all coins and boosters are earned by playing. Nothing can be bought with real money inside the Games.
  • No analytics or tracking: we do not use analytics, tracking or profiling tools of any kind.
  • Works offline: the Games do not need an internet connection to play.

5. Amazon Appstore

Our Games are distributed through the Amazon Appstore. When you find, download or update a Game, Amazon processes information according to its own Amazon Privacy Notice. We do not receive personal information about you from Amazon.

6. Children's privacy

Several of our Games are made to be enjoyed by children and families. Because the Games collect no personal information from anyone, they do not collect personal information from children, in line with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the GDPR.

If you are a parent or guardian and have any question about a Game your child plays, please contact us at any time.

7. When you contact us

The only time we receive personal information is if you choose to email us, for example for support. We then receive your email address and whatever you write in your message. We use it only to answer you, we never share or sell it, and we delete support emails within 24 months.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Since the Games collect nothing, this normally only concerns emails you have sent us.

We do not sell or share personal information. To make a request, email admin-amzn@ossiannagames.com. We will reply within 30 days.

9. Changes to this policy

If we ever change how a Game handles data (for example by adding a feature that needs to collect information), we will update this page and the Game's store listing before that version is released. The “Last updated” date at the top always shows the latest version.
