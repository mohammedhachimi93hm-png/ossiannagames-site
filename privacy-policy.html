1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Ossi Anna Games (“we”, “us”, “our”) handles information in connection with all games and applications we publish on the Amazon Appstore (the “Games”) and our website ossiannagames.com (together, the “Services”).

By downloading, installing or using our Games, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Services.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you give us

If you contact us (for example by email for support), we receive your email address and any information you choose to include in your message. We only use it to answer you.

2.2 Information collected automatically

Depending on the Game, limited technical information may be collected automatically when you play, such as:

  • Device information: device model, operating system (Fire OS) version, language and screen size;
  • Game usage information: levels played, session length, in-game events and settings;
  • Diagnostic information: crash reports and performance data, used to fix bugs;
  • Advertising identifier (only in Games that show ads — see section 6).

Game progress and settings are usually stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the Game normally deletes this data.

2.3 Information we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect your name, postal address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or payment card details.

3. How we use information

  • To provide, operate and maintain the Games;
  • To fix bugs, improve performance and understand which features players enjoy;
  • To show advertising in Games that include ads;
  • To answer your support requests;
  • To comply with legal obligations and prevent fraud or abuse.

4. In-app purchases

Some Games may offer optional in-app purchases. All payments are processed by Amazon through the Amazon Appstore and Amazon In-App Purchasing. We never receive or store your payment card or billing details; we only receive a confirmation that a purchase was completed so we can unlock the item. Amazon's handling of your data is described in the Amazon Privacy Notice.

6. Advertising

Some of our Games are free and supported by ads. Advertising partners may use your device's advertising identifier to show ads and measure their performance. You can limit this at any time:

  • Fire tablets: Settings → Privacy → Advertising ID → turn off “Interest-based Ads”, or reset your Advertising ID;
  • Fire TV: Settings → Preferences → Privacy Settings → turn off “Interest-based Ads”.

Turning off interest-based ads does not remove ads, but the ads you see will be less personalized.

7. Children's privacy

Our Games are designed to be enjoyed by a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the minimum age required in your country, such as 16 in some EU countries) without verifiable parental consent, in line with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the GDPR.

In any Game directed at children, we do not show personalized (interest-based) advertising. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Data retention

We keep information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy. Support emails are kept for up to 24 months and then deleted. Data held by third-party services is kept according to their own retention policies.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you;
  • Ask us to correct or delete it;
  • Object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent at any time;
  • Receive your data in a portable format;
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, except through the advertising services described above, which you can limit in your device settings.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at admin-amzn@ossiannagames.com with the subject “Data request” and the name of the Game. We will reply within 30 days.

11. International transfers
